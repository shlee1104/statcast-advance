Sequencing

Frequency and effectiveness are measured separately. A sequence that is predictable and not working is the most exploitable pattern a report can carry, and any metric that merges the two hides it.

freq_lift is how much more likely the follow-up becomes; effect_lift is whether the whiff rate improves. High freq_lift with effect_lift at or below 1.0 is the finding.
